How these numbers are worked out

Hit chance
= d20 faces that hit / 20
= (21 − (AC − attack bonus)) / 20, kept between 1 and 19 faces
A natural 1 always misses and a natural 20 always hits, so it is always 1 to 19 faces: 5% to 95%.
Expected per round
= P(normal hit) × (avg die + D) + P(crit) × (2 × avg die + D)

+1 to hit
= (avg die + D) / 20 per round: one more d20 face becomes a normal hit. 0 once capped.
+1 to damage
= P(hit) per round: every hit deals 1 more.
Which wins
+1 to hit wins only while avg die + D > 20 × P(hit).

Try this

Each button sets the controls at the top of the page, and every table, chart and figure follows. Read what to look for, then use the links below to go and see it.

  • A crossover

    At AC 25 only a natural 20 hits, so +1 to hit buys nothing until +6. After that, each +1 to hit adds a whole extra d12 hit (6.5 over 20 rounds), while +1 to damage adds just 1.0. Look for the ring at +6, where +N to hit takes over.

    Sets: AC 25, d12, +0 to hit, +0 damage, compare at +6

  • Damage always wins

    At AC 15 with +0 to hit you expect 6 hits in 20 rounds, so each +1 to damage adds 6.0. Each +1 to hit adds one more hit, worth only 4.5. The dashed line stays on top at every N, and there is no ring.

    Sets: AC 15, d8, +0 to hit, +0 damage, compare at +5

  • The cap flips it

    With a d12, one more hit is worth 6.5 and +1 to damage adds 6.0, so +N to hit leads at first. At +13 it reaches the 95% cap and goes flat, and +N to damage passes it at +15.

    Sets: AC 15, d12, +0 to hit, +0 damage, compare at +15
