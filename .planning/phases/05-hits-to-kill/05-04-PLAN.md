## Phase Goal

**As a** game designer, **I want to** set an enemy's hit points (starting from a 40 HP enemy, by exact amount or by whole average hits of my attack) and see exactly how many hits and rounds it takes to kill it and how likely a kill is within 20 rounds, **so that** I can judge an attack by how fast it kills, not only by its average damage.

<objective>
Final slice: the 20-round roll fights the enemy. After a roll, the log shows the enemy's HP falling round by round, marks the round it dies, and dims the rounds after it. A kill line reports the result. The tally lists each run's outcome. Editing Enemy HP re-scores the same dice live, with no re-roll and no stale state. Then the whole phase is verified.

Purpose: KILL-05 and KILL-06 end to end, and the phase verification against ROADMAP SC1–SC4, D-01..D-12 and KILL-01..06.
Output: index.html (VM re-scoring, log column, kill line, tally of runs, CSS, smoke), tools/check-engine.mjs (K8), and 05-04-SUMMARY.md with the phase mapping.

Out of scope: counting kills per 20 rounds ("next enemy steps in"), kill speed in the compare tables or chart, and a distribution chart. All are deferred. Sim.simulate and the draw order are unchanged: seeds 1 → 47, 5 → 54 and 6 → 71 stay pinned. Do not push, add remotes, or run gh. Commit locally only; the user pushes on request.
</objective>
