## Phase Goal

**As a** game designer, **I want to** set an enemy's hit points (starting from a 40 HP enemy, by exact amount or by whole average hits of my attack) and see exactly how many hits and rounds it takes to kill it and how likely a kill is within 20 rounds, **so that** I can judge an attack by how fast it kills, not only by its average damage.

<objective>
First slice: the page gets an enemy. The designer types an Enemy HP from 1 to 500, starting at 40, and three exact kill cards appear under a live "Killing a 40 HP enemy" heading: hits to kill, rounds to kill with the median round, and the chance of a kill within 20 rounds. The numbers are right at every setting, including the extremes.

Purpose: KILL-01 and KILL-03 end to end. The engine is the research's validated thinning DP. Tie-safe rounding and the edge copy land in this plan, so no card ever prints a wrong digit.
Output: index.html (engine, view-model, markup, CSS, renderer, self-test pins, UI smoke), tools/check-page.mjs (guard 11 list), and 05-01-SUMMARY.md.

Out of scope for this plan: the ±1 hit buttons and the hits line (05-03), the kill note (05-02), exhaustive check-engine proofs (05-02), and the 20-round roll (05-04). Deferred ideas (kill speed in the compare tables, a rounds-to-kill chart mode, a distribution chart, and "next enemy steps in") are never built. Do not push, add remotes, or run gh: every push to main redeploys the live site. Commit locally only.
</objective>
