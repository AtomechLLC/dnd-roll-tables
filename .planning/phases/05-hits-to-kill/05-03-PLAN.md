## Phase Goal

**As a** game designer, **I want to** set an enemy's hit points (starting from a 40 HP enemy, by exact amount or by whole average hits of my attack) and see exactly how many hits and rounds it takes to kill it and how likely a kill is within 20 rounds, **so that** I can judge an attack by how fast it kills, not only by its average damage.

<objective>
Third slice: the designer grows or shrinks the enemy one average hit at a time. Two buttons beside the HP box step by the attack's average damage on a hit, rounded to whole HP. Each button shows what it will do, and a line under the box translates the HP into average hits. At the bounds, and for attacks too weak to round to 1 HP, the buttons go inert honestly.

Purpose: KILL-02 end to end, and the rest of KILL-01's accessibility contract (tab order, names, enable and disable).
Output: index.html (VM step and status strings, markup, CSS, click handler, renderer, init / failPage wiring, smoke), tools/check-engine.mjs (K7), tools/check-page.mjs (guards 7 and 11, fault modes), and 05-03-SUMMARY.md.

Out of scope: presets touching HP (they keep PRESET_KEYS ac, die, atk, dmg, n per CONTEXT discretion), the 20-round roll (05-04), and anything in the compare tables or chart (D-12). Do not push, add remotes, or run gh. Commit locally only.
</objective>
