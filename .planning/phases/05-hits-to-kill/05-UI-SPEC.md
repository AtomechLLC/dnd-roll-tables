# Phase 5 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for frontend phases. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.
> **This contract extends `01-UI-SPEC.md` … `04-UI-SPEC.md` as amended by quick tasks 260927-17o (page order) and 260927-1ty (density).** Everything they lock stays: fonts, the 4-size / 2-weight type scale, every color token, the accent reserved list, table tiers, legend classes, `.card` / `.section-intro` / `.explain` patterns, the Roll and preset buttons, the focus ring, and the page order. **Spacing follows the dense layout, not the older specs:** tokens `--sp-1..6` = 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 20 / 24px, and controls are 32px tall.
> Sources: 05-CONTEXT.md (D-01..D-12, locked), ROADMAP Phase 5 success criteria 1–4, REQUIREMENTS KILL-01..06, the two quick-task summaries, 04-REVIEW-FIX.md (claims are checked against the engine), and `index.html` (style block, `form#controls`, `section#stats`, `section#sim`, `DiceMath`, `Sim`, `VM.statsView`, `renderSim`). **Every reference number below was computed this session by running the page's own `#dice-math`, `#dice-sim` and `#view-model` blocks in Node, with an exact DP over remaining HP.** None was typed by hand. - **Widths:** at 1366px the five groups need about 1,221px and the strip's inner width is 1,308px, so everything stays on one row, and the strip grows by the hits line (about 24px). On narrower laptops the HP group wraps to a second strip row. At 360 / 390px the group is 302 / 332px wide, so `.hp-row` wraps: the box on line 1, both buttons together on line 2 (`.hp-steps` never splits), then the hits line. The page never scrolls sideways.

## Component Contract: Enemy HP control (KILL-01, KILL-02, D-01..D-03)

**One parse path (CTRL-05):** new `VM.CONTROL_SPEC.hp = { min: 1, max: 500 }` and `VM.DEFAULT_STATE.hp = 40`. The box goes through `onControl` / `VM.parseControl` like every other number box: blank or non-numeric keeps the previous value, out of range clamps, the committed box normalizes on `change`, and a focused box is never overwritten while typing.

**The step (D-02, D-03):**
- `avgHit = e20Num / (e20Den × hitFaces)`: the exact "Average damage on a hit" card value (crits included). hitFaces ≥ 1 always (a natural 20 always hits), so it never divides by zero.
- `step = round-half-up(avgHit)`. Engine scan (all 70,980 attack settings): rounding the exact value and rounding the card's 2-decimal string agree at **every** setting (0 mismatches). 99 settings are exact `.5` ties, which go up (a card showing `x.50` steps up). **1,412 settings have step 0** (average hit under 0.5 HP, for example AC 15 / d4 / +5 / −4: `0.11`).
- `applied−` = min(step, hp − 1), `applied+` = min(step, 500 − hp). The label shows the change the click **will actually make**, so it never lies near a bound.

| Case | `#hp-minus` label | `#hp-plus` label | Inert (`aria-disabled="true"`) |
|------|-------------------|------------------|--------------------------------|
| Normal (defaults, HP 40, step 5) | `−1 hit (−5 HP)` | `+1 hit (+5 HP)` | neither |
| Clamped near a bound (HP 3) | `−1 hit (−2 HP)` | `+1 hit (+5 HP)` | neither |
| At the bound (HP 1 / HP 500) | `−1 hit (0 HP)` | `+1 hit (0 HP)` | the one whose applied change is 0 |
| Step 0 (AC 15 / d4 / +5 / −4) | `−1 hit (0 HP)` | `+1 hit (0 HP)` | both |

- A click on an active button sends `hp ± applied` through `VM.parseControl('hp', …, state.hp)` and one `setState({ hp })`. An inert button ignores activation. It can't loop, can't do nothing silently while claiming a step, and never divides by the step.
- **Inert uses `aria-disabled="true"`, never `disabled` [discretion]:** the button keeps focus, so a keyboard user who presses `−1 hit` down to HP 1 is not dropped to `<body>`, and the guard-11 tab order stays fixed. The `disabled` attribute is used only in the markup before init and after `failPage` (with Roll and the presets).
- Style: see Step button states in Color. Font `400 0.875rem/1.4 var(--font-num)`, `tabular-nums`, `white-space: nowrap`, radius 4px, `cursor: pointer; touch-action: manipulation;`. No transitions.

**The hits line `#hp-hits` (D-03):** `simpleHits = hp / avgHit`, formatted by the kill number format (below). It is **the same string** as the Hits card's "simple division says" number.

| Case | Copy |
|------|------|
| Step ≥ 1 | `≈ 8.1 average hits (1 hit ≈ 4.91 HP)` |
| Step 0 | `≈ 352.0 average hits (1 hit ≈ 0.11 HP, which rounds to 0 HP, so the hit buttons are off)` |
| simpleHits under 0.05 (for example HP 1, AC 10 / d12 / +20 / +20) | `under 0.1 average hits (1 hit ≈ 26.84 HP)` |

`4.91` / `0.11` / `26.84` are always the Average-damage card's `avgPerHitText`.

## Component Contract: Kill cards and note (KILL-03, KILL-04, D-04..D-07)

### The math behind the strings (exact, no Monte Carlo)

All from one per-attack damage distribution, enumerated through `DiceMath.damageFor` with a true 2dX crit (RULES-05). Death is HP ≤ 0.
- **Hits to kill** `E[H]`: the exact expected number of hits, over the per-hit distribution (conditional on hitting, 0-damage hits included). Recursion over remaining HP, `O(hp × support)`. No cutoff needed.
- **Rounds to kill** `E[R]`: the same over the per-round distribution (misses deal 0). Check: `E[R] × hitFaces = 20 × E[H]` (Wald). At the defaults 8.700 × 20 / 11 = 15.818.
- **Median round:** the first t with P(dead by round t) ≥ 0.5, found by a round-by-round DP over remaining HP. It stops at **`KILL_MEDIAN_CAP = 1000` rounds** (the D-06 documented cutoff). Past the cap, the copy says so and prints no number.
- **Kill within 20:** P(dead by the end of round 20), the same DP for 20 rounds.
- **Fastest kill** `ceil(hp / (2 × die + D))`. `2 × die + D` is the best crit, always ≥ 3, and a natural 20 can come every round.

### Number formats (one formatter each, shared by card, hits line, note and status)

| Quantity | Rule | Examples |
|----------|------|----------|
| Hits, rounds, simple division | Under 1,000: one decimal. 1,000 or more: a whole number with a comma. Simple division under 0.05: `under 0.1`. | `8.7`, `652.0`, `1,296`, `15,215` |
| Median / fastest round, cap | Whole number, comma at 1,000 or more | `15`, `1,000` |
| Kill within 20 | `0%` **iff fastest > 20** (an integer test, never a float compare). Otherwise p < 0.001 → `<0.1%`; p < 0.01 → one decimal (`0.2%`); p < 0.99 → whole (`85%`); p < 0.999 → one decimal (`99.4%`); else `>99.9%`. **Never `100%`:** a natural 1 can miss every round, so a kill is never certain. | `85%`, `0.2%`, `<0.1%`, `>99.9%`, `0%` |

### Cards

| Card | Label | Value | Sub-line (Small 12, muted) |
|------|-------|-------|----------------------------|
| 1 | `Hits to kill` | `E[H]` | `on average (simple division says 8.1)`. **Every hit kills** (integer test: `2 + D ≥ hp` and, when hitFaces ≥ 2, `1 + D ≥ hp`; then E[H] = 1 exactly): `every hit kills (simple division says 0.2)` |
| 2 | `Rounds to kill` | `E[R]` | `on average, half of fights by round 15`. Past the cap: `on average; half of fights last over 1,000 rounds` |
| 3 | `Kill within 20 rounds` | P(kill ≤ 20) | `fastest possible: round 3` |

**Heading:** `Killing a {hp} HP enemy`.

### Kill note `#kill-note` (D-07; Body, `.explain`, three sentences)

Every number is a card or control string: `{hp}` = the HP box, `{simple}` / `{EH}` = card 1, `{hitPct}` = the Hit chance card, `{ER}` = card 2.

| Sentence | Copy (defaults) |
|----------|-----------------|
| 1, exact shows higher (every sampled setting) | `Simple division (40 HP ÷ the average hit) says 8.1 hits, but the exact average is 8.7.` |
| 1, displayed tie (defensive; 0 of 4,020 samples) | `Simple division (40 HP ÷ the average hit) says 8.1 hits, and the exact average rounds to the same 8.1.` |
| 2, always | `The enemy drops only on the hit that takes it to 0 HP or below, and whatever that last hit deals past 0 is wasted, so on average the hits needed add up to more than 40 HP.` |
| 3, always | `Misses come between the hits: at a 55% hit chance, the kill takes 15.8 rounds on average.` |

- **Why sentence 2 makes no frequency claim [discretion]:** "the last hit usually overshoots" is **false at about 3% of settings** (engine scan: 2,452 of 81,120 sampled setting × HP pairs have P(overshoot) < 0.5; the lowest is 0.118 at AC 10 / d4 / +10 / −3, HP 1). What is always true is Wald's identity: expected damage dealt by the kill = E[H] × avg hit > HP, because some overshoot is always possible (the best crit is at least 3). So the sentence states the mechanism, never how often.
- The note never prints `HP ÷ 4.91 = …` as an equation. Dividing by the **rounded** card value changes the tenths at about 8% of sampled settings (159,585 of 1.9M), so a reader's hand check would fail. The divisor is named in words.

## Component Contract: 20-round roll additions (KILL-05, KILL-06, D-08..D-11)

**Re-scoring is a pure function** `killTrack(run.rounds, hp)`. It returns, per round, the HP left (`max(0, …)`), the kill round `K` (or null), the hits up to and including K (rounds with result `hit` or `crit`, 0-damage hits included, matching E[H]), and the HP left after round 20. No die is re-rolled. Tally entries store their rounds so they can be re-scored (D-10).

### Log (`#sim-log`)

| Part | Spec |
|------|------|
| New header (6th) | `<th scope="col"><span aria-hidden="true">Enemy HP</span><span class="vh">Enemy HP left</span></th>`, width 88px, right-aligned |
| All cells | `padding: 0 var(--sp-1)` (was `--sp-2`). The widths in Spacing all fit with this padding. Everything else is unchanged (24px rows, num tabular, Result left-aligned prose). |
| Rows before K | HP left after the round: `35` (num 400, right-aligned) |
| Kill row K (`tr.kill`) | HP cell `<b class="num">0</b> <span aria-hidden="true">←</span> <i>killed</i>` (reads "0 killed"; `i` is prose italic 700). **Every cell in the row is 700**, with a 2px `--ink` rule under it: `box-shadow: inset 0 -2px 0 0 var(--ink)` on each cell (inset, like the outcome table's threshold rule, so moving it never shifts layout). No fill and no accent. The non-color cues are weight, the rule and the word. |
| Rows after K (`tr.down`) | HP cell `enemy down` (Small 12, prose italic 400, `white-space: nowrap`). **Every cell `--ink-muted`**, including a `crit` word. The dice, damage and total still count (D-08). |
| Survived (no K) | Every row shows a number. No `tr.kill`, no `tr.down`. |
| `tfoot` | `Total` (colspan 4) · damage total · **HP left after round 20** (`0` if killed, e.g. `2` if not). All 700, as today. |
| Rebuild policy | Rows are built once per `run.id` (Phase 3 cache). An HP change updates only the HP cells' content, the `kill` / `down` classes and the tfoot HP cell **in place**. It never rebuilds rows, and no transitions (SC3, no flicker). |

### Kill line `#sim-kill` (`.sim-line`)

| Case | Copy |
|------|------|
| Killed | `Killed in round <b class="num">12</b> (<b class="num">9</b> hits).` (`1 hit` singular) |
| Survived | `Survived with <b class="num">2</b> HP left.` |

Hidden until a run exists. In a stale run it turns `--ink-muted` like `#sim-place` (`#sim.is-stale #sim-kill`). It re-scores live with HP in both states.

### Tally (`#sim-tally`)

`Last 3 runs at these settings, newest first: **61** · killed R12; 38 · 2 HP left; 57 · killed R15 (expected 54.0)`

- An entry is `{total} · killed R{K}` or `{total} · {left} HP left` (D-10 wording). **Entries are separated by `; ` [discretion]** (was ` · `), because `·` now sits inside each entry.
- The newest **total** stays bold (`b.num`). Outcomes are prose 400. It still shows only for 2–5 fresh runs and clears when the attack changes (Phase 3 D-10 / D-13). Outcomes re-score live with HP (D-10).

### Staleness (D-09)

`Sim.sameAttack` is unchanged (AC, die, attack, damage), so **HP never stales a run**. Editing HP (box or step buttons) re-scores the log, the kill line, the tfoot and the tally in the same frame. The rolled-at note, the `Now:` line and the caption suffix never mention HP. The outcome-table marks are unchanged (D-11).

## Interaction Contract

| Event | Behavior |
|-------|----------|
| Page load | HP box `40`. Step buttons `−1 hit (−5 HP)` / `+1 hit (+5 HP)`, enabled in init's final step together with Roll and the presets. Hits line, heading, kill cards and note are painted by the first guarded render. `#sim-kill` hidden. |
| Type in the HP box | Live on `input` through `parseControl`. Cards, heading, note, hits line, step labels and a rolled run re-score in one rAF frame. No announcement. |
| Click `−1 hit` / `+1 hit` (active) | One `setState({ hp })`. Focus stays on the button, and the page does not scroll. `#hp-status` is cleared, then written in the next rAF (the `#sim-status` pattern, so repeated clicks re-announce). |
| Click an inert step button | Nothing happens and nothing is announced. The inert state is already read out on focus. |
| AC / die / attack / damage change | The step, labels and hits line update (the average hit changes). A rolled run goes stale as today. The kill cards follow. |
| Compare `+N` slider | No effect on anything in this phase. Kill values are memoized per `(ac, die, atk, dmg, hp)`, so an N drag recomputes nothing. |
| Preset click | Presets **do not touch HP [discretion]**. `PRESET_KEYS` stays `ac, die, atk, dmg, n`, and the step and kill cards follow the new attack. |
| Roll 20 rounds | As today, plus the HP column, the kill row, `#sim-kill` and the tally outcome. `#sim-status` gains the kill sentence. |
| Motion | None. No transitions or animation, so reduced motion needs no handling. |
| Performance | The whole kill view (E[H], E[R], median ≤ cap, P20) must render within one frame at the worst settings. The planner times it in check-engine at HP 500 across the slowest settings (low-damage d4 / d8 with a penalty, nat-20-only). If it takes more than 16 ms, lower `KILL_MEDIAN_CAP` (1000 → 500 → 200) and keep the copy templated on the cap. |

## Handoff Notes for the Planner

- **CSS (existing `<style>`):** `#controls { align-items: flex-start; }`, `#controls fieldset.dice legend { margin-bottom: var(--sp-1); }`, `.hp-row`, `.hp-steps`, `.hp-hits`, `.btn-step` (+ hover, active, `[aria-disabled="true"]`, `:disabled`), `#stats > .stats-h`, `.kill-note` (+ the `@container page (width >= 64rem)` rule), `.sim-log` padding 4px and the new widths (36 / 36 / 52 / 60 / 44 / 88, total 316), `.sim-log thead th:nth-child(6)`, `tr.kill > *`, `tr.down > *` (incl. `.res-crit`), `.hp-down`, `.hp-kill i`, `#sim.is-stale #sim-kill`.
- **View-model:** `CONTROL_SPEC.hp`, `DEFAULT_STATE.hp = 40`. A pure `VM.killView(p, hp, statsView)` memoized per `(ac, die, atk, dmg, hp)` returns `{ step, minusApplied, plusApplied, hitsLine, heading, cards[3], note[3], statusText }`, all strings, using the formats above. A pure `VM.killTrack(rounds, hp)` is used by the log, the kill line, the tally and the Roll status. **The engine piece (the per-hit / per-round pmf, the E[H] / E[R] recursion, the round DP with the cap) belongs in `#dice-math`**, with the one damage rule.
- **check-engine:** pin the D-05 values (E[H] 8.700, E[R] 15.818, medians 9 / 15, mins 3 / 3, P20 0.8466, P10 0.1114). Check Wald `E[R] × hitFaces = 20 × E[H]` at every sampled setting. Check that step-from-exact equals step-from-card on all 70,980 settings (expect 0 mismatches; 1,412 step-0 settings; 99 exact ties round up). Check that the displayed E[H] is greater than the displayed simple division (expect the tie branch count 0), that "every hit kills" ⇔ E[H] = 1, that `0%` ⇔ fastest > 20, that no percent string is `100%`, that the hits line number equals the card's simple number, and that every note number equals its card string (checked against the engine, not a rebuilt string). Sample HP at 1, 2, 3, 5, 40, 45, 100, 500 and at multiples of the average hit ± 1. Optionally cross-check a few settings with BigInt rationals to confirm the displayed tenths.
- **In-page `?test`** is at its 200 ms budget. Add O(1) pins only: the default card strings and one `killTrack` on a hand-made 3-round record.
- **UI smoke / check-page:** update guard 11's list (the three new ids after `#dmg-num`), the Phase 3 tally expectation (`; ` separator and outcomes), and the Roll status expectation. `--expect` at defaults: `Enemy HP`, `−1 hit (−5 HP)`, `+1 hit (+5 HP)`, `≈ 8.1 average hits (1 hit ≈ 4.91 HP)`, `Killing a 40 HP enemy`, `on average (simple division says 8.1)`, `on average, half of fights by round 15`, `fastest possible: round 3`, `the exact average is 8.7`, `the kill takes 15.8 rounds on average`. A seeded smoke check: a run re-scores when `hp` changes without `is-stale` and without a new `run.id`, and `tr.kill` moves.
- **Manual gate:** look at 1366px and 360px in both color schemes at the defaults, HP 1, HP 500, step 0 and after a roll (killed and survived). Check that the control tops line up, the buttons wrap as a pair on phones, the kill note sits beside the cards at 1366, the log is 316px with no truncated header, and `enemy down` and `0 ← killed` fit.
