## Phase Goal

**As a** game designer, **I want to** set an enemy's hit points (starting from a 40 HP enemy, by exact amount or by whole average hits of my attack) and see exactly how many hits and rounds it takes to kill it and how likely a kill is within 20 rounds, **so that** I can judge an attack by how fast it kills, not only by its average damage.

<objective>
Second slice: the kill cards gain their explanation and their proof. The designer reads a three-sentence note under the cards (beside them on a laptop). It says why 40 HP ÷ 4.91 = 8.1 undersells the real 8.7 hits: the last hit's damage past 0 is wasted. Behind it, check-engine proves every kill number and every note number at every setting against independent exact oracles.

Purpose: KILL-04 end to end, and the exhaustive half of KILL-03 ("no hang or wrong number at extreme settings"). The Phase 4 lesson applies: copy claims are checked against the engine, never against a rebuilt string.
Output: index.html (note copy, markup, CSS, renderer, one self-test pin, smoke), tools/check-engine.mjs (K1..K6), and 05-02-SUMMARY.md.

Out of scope: the ±1 hit buttons and hits line (05-03), the 20-round roll (05-04), and any change to the compare tables or chart (D-12). The formulas card gets no hits-to-kill line (UI-SPEC A10). Do not push, add remotes, or run gh. Commit locally only.
</objective>
