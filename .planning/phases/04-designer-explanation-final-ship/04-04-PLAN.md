## Phase Goal

**As a** game designer who has never seen the page, **I want to** read it top to bottom and understand why attack bonuses saturate at the 5%–95% clamp while damage bonuses scale linearly, with text that always matches the live numbers, **so that** I can reason about bonus design from the finished, accessible page at its public URL.

<objective>
