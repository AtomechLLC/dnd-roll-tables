## Phase Goal

**As a** game designer who has never seen the page, **I want to** read it top to bottom and understand why attack bonuses saturate at the 5%–95% clamp while damage bonuses scale linearly, with text that always matches the live numbers, **so that** I can reason about bonus design from the finished, accessible page at its public URL.

<objective>
Second slice: the page explains its own numbers. A designer reads, next to the outcome table, why the table is the whole outcome space of two rolls (COPY-01), and, next to the stat cards, the formula behind hit chance, expected damage per round and the value of the next +1, with today's values substituted and the card's own result at the end (COPY-02). Every branch is chosen by exact integer comparison, so no formula ever prints a number that disagrees with a card (D-04).

Purpose: COPY-01 and COPY-02 complete. The copy lives in pure VM functions of a statsView result, so check-engine asserts it on all 70,980 settings without new engine calls.
Output: index.html (VM builders, markup, CSS, renderers, 3 self-test pins, smoke block) and tools/check-engine.mjs ((r), (s), (t), (z)).

Deviations from 04-UI-SPEC.md to record in the SUMMARY (both verified by RESEARCH on the full domain):
1. 'Which wins' uses the inequality form only when both +1 lines are rule branch. At 80 settings (nat-20-only, D = −2, e.g. AC 15 / d8 / −5 / −2) '+1 to hit' is table branch and '+1 to damage' is rule branch; the UI-SPEC's inequality would print 2.5 where the real value is 2.625, so the compare-displayed verdict is used there (RESEARCH Pitfall 1).
2. The two-roll sentence uses 'an' before 8, 11 and 18 (RESEARCH Pitfall 3); the UI-SPEC shows only 'a 10'.

No push, gh, or Pages work (user memory). Edit index.html and tools/check-engine.mjs with the Edit or Write tool, never a Bash heredoc (RESEARCH Pitfall 10).
</objective>
