## Phase Goal

**As a** game designer, **I want to** pick a bonus N and see, cell by cell and as a curve, how +N on the attack roll compares with +N on the damage roll, **so that** I can see where the attack bonus plateaus and whether the two curves cross at all at the current AC and die.

<objective>
Make the chart honest about the two things the lesson hinges on: where the attack curve goes flat (95% cap, nat-20-only floor) and whether the curves cross at all. Add the crossing detector and band helper to the engine, render hollow crossing markers and shaded, labeled bands, complete the generated caption, then run the phase-level verification and align the two stale ROADMAP success criteria.

Purpose: This is the payoff sentence of the page: at most settings the damage bonus simply wins, and where it does not, the chart marks exactly where the order flips.
Output: index.html (engine helpers, VM, renderer, CSS, legend, checks), tools/check-engine.mjs (m, n, distribution), ROADMAP.md SC1/SC3 wording.

Out of scope: the WR-01 fix, a band or note for the d4 no-damage flat line (Phase 4 copy), any push.
</objective>
