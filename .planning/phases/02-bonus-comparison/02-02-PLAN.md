## Phase Goal

**As a** game designer, **I want to** pick a bonus N and see, cell by cell and as a curve, how +N on the attack roll compares with +N on the damage roll, **so that** I can see where the attack bonus plateaus and whether the two curves cross at all at the current AC and die.

<objective>
Deliver the curve half of the lesson: the SVG sweep chart with both series, labeled axes and nice ticks, the N marker driven by the slider from plan 02-01, direct labels, an HTML legend, a generated caption (settings, start value, and who leads where), and the hover readout.

Purpose: The designer sees the attack curve bend flat while the damage line keeps climbing, and can read any N exactly.
Output: index.html chart section, VM chart block, renderer, CSS tiers; tools/check-engine.mjs series invariants.

This plan leaves the chart's crossing markers, shaded flat bands, and the caption's crossing-count and band sentences to plan 02-03, which fills four empty SVG groups this plan creates in paint order. Out of scope: click/drag to set N (deferred by the user), the WR-01 per-round fix, any push.
</objective>
